## Pending acceptance / limits

Native /usr/bin/chromium exited before opening a page; no bypass. Native paint/layout/narrow panels, touch/drag smoothness, screen-reader descriptions, Windows and long-run/performance checks remain pending. DOM/runtime and mathematical checks do not certify these.

Locator is informational, not an inset navigation control. Small extents use enlarged minimum markers; dateline pieces can therefore visually exaggerate tiny areas. A padding-only viewport removes focus outline and explains why. Bounding viewport orientation only, not a legal boundary or area/distance tool. Supported projections are cylindrical; future projections require separate inversion support. Static/no-JS/media output preserves authored extent, and story controls remain complete-view-only. No wheel/pinch zoom or out-of-crop recovery is added. No full competitor parity claim.
