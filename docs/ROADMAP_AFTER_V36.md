# OpenViz Studio — quality-first roadmap after V3.6

**Proposals, not commitments.** The product remains first and foremost a professional, freely accessible data visualisation platform. Refine its depth and coherence rather than launching more unfinished workspaces.

| Milestone | Main goal | What “good” looks like |
|---|---|---|
| V3.6.1 | First-time user feedback and fixes | Import-to-finished-chart works without prior training; no accidental sample data or ambiguous actions |
| V3.7 | Chart editor UX and visual quality | Consistent sensible typography, labels, chart-specific settings, preview behaviour and presets across existing chart types |
| V3.8 | Chart catalogue, mapping and comparison quality | Clear examples, accurate auto-mapping, informative errors, transparent aggregation and reliable recommendations |
| V3.9 | Accessibility, reliability and export polish | Real-browser saving/refresh verification; keyboard/screen-reader audits; strong SVG/PDF/PPTX/HTML fidelity |
| V4.0 candidate | Small public beta after evidence-based acceptance | Usability sessions, data-integrity tests, cross-browser matrix, docs, privacy/security and stable deployment workflow |

**Deferred unless strong user demand emerges:** audio multitrack editing, a complete video editor, spreadsheet automation, account systems, AI model integration, adding another dozen unrefined chart types. Existing animation, Story Studio and presentations are maintained as supporting visualisation outputs, not the product centre.
