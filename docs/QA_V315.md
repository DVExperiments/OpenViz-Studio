## Data and asset semantics

Category assignments key by exact category value; changing data does not guess replacements. Unused assignments can be pruned. Flags are manually selected or matched by exact normalised English name/ISO2 plus documented aliases; automatic matching is not geographic inference. Images are decorative and do not change values, sorting, missing-data policy or aggregation. Custom PNGs are header/budget checked on import; this is not a complete PNG CRC decoder. Native uploads decode and resize before storage.

Input limit: 5 MiB, PNG/JPEG/WebP, 8,192 pixels per side and 16 MP decoded. Output: PNG ≤128 pixels per side and ≤80 KiB each; 2 MiB custom-image budget per chart. Remote URLs/SVG are disallowed. Invalid imported assets are ignored; over-budget custom images hidden. The manager supports removing invalid assignments.

Bundled flags derive from flag-icons 7.6.0 under MIT; licence and generator are included. Team badges are original illustrative assets, not real organisation logos.
