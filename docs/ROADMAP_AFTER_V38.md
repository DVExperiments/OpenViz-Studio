# Roadmap after V3.8

1. Browser acceptance of the staged source-switch flow, field requirements, examples and calculation controls, including different-schema datasets and keyboard/narrow-screen use.
2. Real-origin save/reopen, refresh and .openviz round-trip acceptance, plus chart/export comparisons.
3. V3.9 proposal: accessibility, cross-browser reliability and PDF/PPTX/HTML fidelity. Use actual user findings to prioritise.
4. Continue calculation/data-integrity review across specialised renderers; do not infer a full statistical audit from V3.8's targeted blank-value fixes.
5. Public beta only after documented usability, accuracy, persistence and security acceptance.

The approved chart editor structure remains stable. No accounts, AI model requirement, unrelated audio/video workspace or chart-count expansion is planned by default.
