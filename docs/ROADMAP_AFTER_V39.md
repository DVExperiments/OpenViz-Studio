# Roadmap after V3.9

1. User review and browser/export acceptance of Templates and the first expansion batch.
2. Refine series styling, legends, dense labels, long category names and responsive facets before expanding counts again.
3. Survey/research: Likert/diverging response charts, empirical CDF, uncertainty intervals and other forms only with explicit statistical definitions.
4. Experimental: evaluate Evidence lanes/Change lens against conventional alternatives. Prototype assumption sensitivity and missingness analysis if they help users understand data.
5. Re-audit family/variant coverage against current Flourish documentation. Do not equate our 36 selectable entries with 36 distinct families or claim overall quality superiority without comparative evidence.

Later business/hierarchy/spatial and data-driven race work stays behind data integrity, accessibility and export quality gates.
