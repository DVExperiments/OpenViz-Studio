## Pending and limits

Native browser/font layout, Windows persistence, screen readers and performance on detailed real-world geometry remain unverified. Text boxes use estimated metrics, not browser measurements. Labels may be shortened/omitted; lines can cross features. Candidate order determines priority. No manual placement or collision optimisation beyond bounded candidates. Custom labels include regions with no values; names are not observations. The locator marks the displayed bounding extent, with enlarged tiny markers and antimeridian split, rather than individual observations. It can cover underlying map detail and is optional. Existing local equirectangular projection, topology caveats, geometry and publication budgets remain.
